# examples/AGENTS.md

## Applies To

This card applies to `examples/` and its descendants until a nearer
`AGENTS.md` narrows the route.

## Role

`examples/` is the public-safe, schema-backed examples surface for inspectable
agent-layer contracts. It is not the source-authored canon layer.

## Boundaries

- Keep examples minimal, portable, and aligned with the nearest schema.
- Keep negative fixtures intentional and reviewable.
- Do not add real run history, secrets, private prompts, hidden transport
  assumptions, or runtime authority.
- Use the human [examples index](README.md) for inventory and usage context.

## Validation

Use the repository [validation map](../VALIDATION.md) and the nearest
mechanic or part validator when this route is relevant.
